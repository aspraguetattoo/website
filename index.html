Tattooing since 2010. Navy veteran. Based at Faces In The Dark Tattoo in Kyle, Texas — Hill Country, between Austin and San Antonio. Clients come in from all over Hays County — Kyle, Buda, and San Marcos — and plenty make the short drive down from Austin.

Work leans neotraditional and traditional: heavy outlines, packed color or solid black, readable from across the room. Also takes black & grey realism, portraits, and cover-ups when the idea calls for it.
