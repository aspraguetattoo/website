Booking & timing

When are books open?

Aarron is taking a short break to heal up, and books reopen early December. Requests are being collected now for early December and later. Waitlist folks get first pick of dates for 48 hours before books open publicly.

How soon will I hear back?

Booking requests usually get a reply within 24⁠–⁠72 hours.

How do I send reference photos?

The form doesn’t take uploads. After you submit, email your references to aspraguetattoo@gmail.com or DM them to @aspraguetattoo.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. You get one free reschedule with at least 24⁠–⁠48 hours’ notice before your appointment. After that, a new deposit is required to book a new appointment.

Pricing & payment

How much will my tattoo cost?

The shop minimum is $100. Beyond that, pricing depends on the piece, so talk it through in a consult. For large-scale pieces only, the full-day rate is $1,600 for 8 hours.

How do I pay?

Cash or card.

The shop & the work

Where is the shop?

Faces In The Dark Tattoo, 121 Hall Professional Center, Suite E, Kyle, TX 78640. That’s Hill Country, between Austin and San Antonio, an easy drive from Buda, San Marcos, and Austin. Open in maps.

What are the shop hours?

Tuesday to Saturday 12–8, Sunday 12–6, closed Monday.

What styles does Aarron tattoo?

Work leans neotraditional and traditional: heavy outlines, packed color or solid black, readable from across the room. He also takes black & grey realism and portraits when the idea calls for it. See the work page for examples.

Do you do cover-ups and reworks?

Yes. Cover-ups and reworks are a regular part of the work. Pick “Yes” on the form, and include a photo of the existing tattoo when you send references.

Does Aarron do guest spots?

Yes. Upcoming dates are on the guest spots page.

Your appointment

How old do I need to be?

18 and older only. Bring a passport or state ID.

Can I see my drawing before the appointment?

You’ll see your drawing on the day of your appointment. Drawings aren’t sent ahead of time through DMs, text, or email.

Can I bring a friend?

Sometimes. Ask ahead of time.

Aftercare & touch-ups

How do I take care of a new tattoo?

Follow the aftercare guide. It covers second-skin bandages, regular wraps, the first few weeks, and when to reach out.

Are touch-ups free?

Touch-ups are typically free.
