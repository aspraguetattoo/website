04 / Booking

How to get in.

Books move. Availability can tighten around recovery and travel windows, so write sooner rather than later. Walk-ins are possible if Aarron is at the shop and the day allows it — do not count on that for a large custom piece.

  1. Send the request Email the shop or Aarron directly, or DM @aspraguetattoo. Email is cleaner for custom work.
  2. Include the useful details Idea, size, placement, color vs black & grey, reference images, any existing tattoo or scar in the area, and dates that work for you.
  3. Hold the date Accepted projects take a deposit to lock the appointment. Deposit details come with the reply.
