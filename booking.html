How it works

Four steps to the chair.

  1. Send a request or set up a consult Use the form above, email the shop or Aarron directly, or DM @aspraguetattoo. Not sure yet? Ask for a free consultation.
  2. Talk through the idea Idea, size, placement, color vs black & grey, reference images, any existing tattoo or scar in the area, and dates that work for you.
  3. Pay the $100 deposit to lock the date The non-refundable deposit holds your appointment. See the policies below.
  4. Get tattooed Come in on your date at Faces In The Dark Tattoo in Kyle, then follow the aftercare guide while it heals.
Deposit & policies

The fine print, kept short.

Consultations are free

Not sure about the idea, size, or placement yet? A consultation costs nothing. Set one up through the form above (pick “Free consultation”), by email, or with an Instagram DM.

A $100 deposit holds your appointment

Every booking requires a $100 non-refundable deposit to hold the appointment.

Rescheduling

Life happens. You get one free reschedule with at least 24⁠–⁠48 hours’ notice before your appointment.

After that, a new deposit is required to book a new appointment.
